HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Flight & Physics Controls

• W / ↑ : Fly Upward / Generate Lift (counter gravity).

• S / ↓ : Dive / Fast Downward Descent.

• A / ← : Bank Left | D / → : Bank Right.

• The dragon naturally falls under gravity unless lift is applied!

• On Mobile / Tablet, use the on-screen touch D-Pad.

• P or Esc : Pause / Resume Game.

🌟 Core Gameplay Loop

• Collect Coins (+10 pts each) to increase your score and reach milestones.

• Avoid Wild Birds. Colliding with a sky predator costs 1 Life (❤️).

• Every 50 Coins triggers a General Knowledge Sky Challenge (50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300...).

• Answer correctly within 10 seconds to gain +100 bonus points and continue flying.

• Wrong answers or timeouts reduce 1 Life (❤️).

• Watch out for Wind Zones: Updrafts (↑), Downdrafts (↓), and Crosswinds (→).

• Collect Power-ups: 🛡️ Shield (deflects birds), 🧲 Coin Magnet, ⚡ Speed Boost, ❤️ Extra Life.

• Lose all 3 lives $\rightarrow$ DRAGON DOWN.
